# Code of conduct

Poligome is a community project. Participation should be welcoming, constructive, and safe for people regardless of background, identity, experience, nationality, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and the licenses of datasets and models.
- Accept corrections and make room for contributors with different levels of experience.
- Do not publish another person's private information or confidential data.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to `eduardoafonso1089@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and may result in a warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
